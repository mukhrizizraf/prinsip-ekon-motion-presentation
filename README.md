# Minimum Wage on the Menu

BEEBK1013 Principles of Economics (UUM, A261) group video, Assignment 2.

A paper cutout animation of our 9 minute mini documentary on the minimum wage and Malaysian restaurants. Parts 1 to 4 play each member's own voice with their photo, and the captions follow the recordings. Part 5 (Aleesya) will be added when her recording arrives.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or view it on GitHub Pages:

## Recording it as an MP4 for YouTube

1. Open the page in Chrome or Edge on a computer and wait about 5 seconds so the voices finish loading.
2. Press **Full screen**, then **Play** (or press the space bar).
3. Record the screen with sound: Windows Snipping Tool (Record, with system audio on), Xbox Game Bar (Win + Alt + R) or OBS Studio.
4. The whole video runs about 9:10, including a 12 second credits roll, then stops on "Terima kasih!".
